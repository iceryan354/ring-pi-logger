    Before you sleep

    1. Fully close ViHealth so the ring is free to connect.
    2. Plug the phone in.
    3. Open this page in Bluefy, tap Connect ring and pick your ring.
    4. Leave Bluefy open and tap Dim screen. If the phone still locks, set Auto-Lock to Never for the night.
    5. In the morning tap Stop, then Save CSV.
    Readings are saved on the phone as they arrive, so a reload or crash loses at most the last reading. If the link drops, the page reconnects on its own.
